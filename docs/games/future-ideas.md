# Future Ideas

Games we might add to the pool later. These don't have full rules yet. When one gets picked, it gets its own page with rules.

## Individual (solo)

| Game | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| Sheaf Toss | Use a pitchfork to toss a burlap sack of hay over a raised bar. The bar goes up each round, and you're out after 3 misses. A real Highland Games event. |
| Hammerschlagen | Take turns driving a nail into a stump, one swing each, with the flat side of a hammer. First to sink their nail flush wins. Could be drinking (miss = sip). |
| Gladiator Joust | Two players stand on hay bales or stumps and try to knock each other off with pool noodles. Single elimination. |
| Disc Golf | 5–9 "holes" around the property (trees, posts). Fewest throws wins. Someone builds the course each year. |
| Arm Wrestling | Single elimination bracket, with a loser's bracket for placement. |

## 2-player teams

| Game | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| Horseshoes | Traditional doubles. Ringers 3, leaners 1. Round robin. |
| Ladder Golf | Throw bolas at a 3-rung ladder: top rung 3, middle 2, bottom 1. |
| Bocce | Doubles, closest to the pallino. |
| Charades / Pictionary | One partner acts or draws, the other guesses. Most correct in 60 seconds wins. Good for Friday night. |
| Three-Legged Race | Partners tied together for a sprint or short course. |
