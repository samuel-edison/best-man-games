Team handball, scaled down to 5 v 5 on grass. Think soccer played with your hands: pass, run, and throw the ball into a small goal past the goalkeeper.

## Setup

Uses the shared 5v5 field. See **[Field Setup](field-setup.md)** for the diagram and measurements.

- **Field:** 50 yards long by 25 yards wide, same as Ultimate Frisbee and Flag Football.
- **Goal areas:** the 5-yard end zones.
- **Goals:** one goal centered on each end line, about 3 yards wide and 2 yards tall.
- **Penalty marks:** a chalk dot 7 yards out from the center of each goal (2 yards outside the goal area).

## Rules

### Moving with the ball

- You may take **up to 3 steps** while holding the ball.
- You may hold the ball for **up to 3 seconds**.
- **No dribbling.** The grass makes it impractical, so pass instead. This is our change from standard handball.
- Breaking the 3-step or 3-second rule is a turnover. The other team takes a free throw from that spot.

### Using your body

- You can touch the ball with your hands, arms, head, body, and legs above the knee.
- **No kicking** and no playing the ball below the knee. Only the goalkeeper may use their feet, and only inside the goal area.

### Goal area

- **Only the goalkeeper** may stand in the goal area (the end zone).
- An attacker may **jump into** the goal area to shoot, as long as the ball leaves their hand before they land.
- Defenders may not step into the goal area to block. Doing it to stop a shot gives the other team a penalty throw.
- The goalkeeper may move freely inside the goal area but may not carry the ball out of it.

### Contact

- You may block an opponent with your torso, face to face.
- **No** pushing, holding, tripping, hitting, or grabbing or slapping the ball out of an opponent's hands.
- **Call your own fouls** (see [General Rules](../../rules/index.md#game-day-flow)).
- A foul gives the other team a **free throw** from where it happened. Defenders stand at least 3 yards back.
- A foul that stops a **clear chance to score** gives a **penalty throw**: the shooter throws from the penalty mark, one on one against the goalkeeper. Everyone else stays behind the shooter.

### Restarts

- **Start of each half and after every goal:** throw-off from the center of the midfield line. The team that was just scored on takes it.
- **Ball out over a sideline:** throw-in from that spot by the team that didn't touch it last.
- **Ball out over an end line:**
    - Last touched by the attackers or the goalkeeper: goalkeeper throw from the goal area.
    - Last touched by a defender (not the goalkeeper): throw-in from that corner by the attackers.
- **No stalling:** the team with the ball must keep trying to attack. Holding it back without attacking gives possession to the other team.

### Winning

- Two 12-minute halves. Switch ends at halftime.
- Most goals wins.
- If tied at the end, **next goal wins**.

## Bonus

- 1 point for MVP (most goals). Must be on the winning team.

## Supplies

- Handball or small soft ball you can grip in one hand (a size 2 handball, or a small playground ball)
- 2 small goals, about 3 yards wide by 2 yards tall (pop-up soccer goals or PVC frames)
- Pinnies
- Stopwatch
- Chalk for the field (see [Field Setup](field-setup.md))

## Our changes from standard handball

Standard team handball is 7 v 7 on a 40 by 20 meter court, with a curved 6-meter goal area and dribbling allowed. To fit 5 v 5 on grass with lines we already chalk:

- 4 court players and a goalkeeper per side.
- The field is the shared 50 by 25 yard field (standard is about 44 by 22 yards).
- The goal area is the straight 5-yard end zone instead of the curved 6-meter line.
- No dribbling.
- No 9-meter free-throw line. Free throws are taken from the spot of the foul.
- Shorter halves (12 minutes instead of 30).
