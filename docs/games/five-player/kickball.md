# Kickball

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 5-player teams (5 v 5) |
| **Rounds** | 1 game, 5 innings |
| **Teams** | [Picked Friday night](../../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams) from last year's rankings |
| **Points** | Standard 5v5: 9 per winner · IDP Max: 11 (with bonuses) |
| **Time** | About 1 hour |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2024, 2025 |

## Setup

See **[Field Setup](field-setup.md)** for the shared field diagram and measurements.

- Home plate goes on a field corner. The sideline and end line are the foul lines.
- 60 feet between bases.
- 42 feet from home to the pitcher's mound.
- Bunt line 20 feet from home.

## Rules

- Standard kickball rules, 5 innings.
- **Fielding outs:** catch a kicked ball in the air, or hit a runner with the ball while they're off base.
- **Kicking outs:** you're out after 2 strikes (missed kicks), 3 fouls, or 1 strike and 2 fouls.
- **Bunts:** must pass the 20-foot bunt line or it's a foul.
- **Mercy rule:** an inning ends once the batting team scores 5 runs. No limit in the last inning.

## Bonus

- 1 point to any player who makes a double play, max one per player per game. More than one player can earn it.
- 1 point for a home run, max one per player per game.

## Supplies

- Pinnies, kickball, cones for bases, tape measure

## Year notes

- **2024:** 2 points per winner, and bonuses were worth 2 points each (IDP Max 6).
