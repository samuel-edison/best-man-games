# Field Setup

One chalked field covers all the outdoor 5v5 games. Chalk it once on Friday before dark and reuse it all Saturday. Cones handle the few extras.

On a phone, turn it sideways or pinch to zoom. The diagrams stay sharp at any size.

### What to chalk

| Line | How many | Length | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sidelines | 2 | 50 yd | The long sides |
| End lines | 2 | 25 yd | The short sides |
| Goal lines | 2 | 25 yd | 5 yd in from each end line |
| Midfield | 1 | 25 yd | 25 yd from each end line |
| Dots | 3 | | Center of midfield, plus a penalty dot 7 yd out from the middle of each end line |

**Total: 225 yards of chalk line**, plus three dots.

### Laying it out

1. **Pick the spot.** You need a flat area about 50 by 25 yards, with open grass past one corner for the kickball outfield (see below).
2. **Square the first corner** with the 3-4-5 trick: measure 15 yd along one side and 20 yd along the other. The diagonal between those two points should be exactly 25 yd.
3. **Mark all four corners**, then check the two diagonals. Each should be about **55 yd 2 ft 8 in**. If they match, the field is square.
4. **Chalk the perimeter**: two 50 yd sidelines, two 25 yd end lines.
5. **Chalk the goal lines** 5 yd in from each end line, and **midfield** 25 yd in.
6. **Add the dots**: center of midfield, and 7 yd straight out from the middle of each end line.
7. **Set the handball goals** centered on each end line, just outside the field.

Running a string line between stakes makes long chalk lines much straighter.

## How each game uses it

| Game | Uses |
| --- | --- |
| [Ultimate Frisbee](ultimate-frisbee.md) | Whole field. Catch it in the end zone to score. |
| [Flag Football](flag-football.md) | Whole field. Start at your own goal line (the 5-yard line), first down at midfield (the 25-yard line), score in the end zone. |
| [Handball](handball.md) | Whole field. End zones are the goal areas, goals on the end lines, penalty dots, throw-off from the center dot. |
| [Soccer](soccer.md) | Whole field. Same goals as Handball, kickoff from the center dot. End zones and penalty dots aren't used. |
| [Kickball](kickball.md) | One corner of the field. See [Kickball](#kickball) below. |
| [Dodgeball](dodgeball.md) | Cone box over midfield. See [Midfield games](#midfield-games) below. |
| [Tug of War](tug-of-war.md) | Rope centered on midfield. See [Midfield games](#midfield-games) below. |
| [Beerball-Baseball](beerball-baseball.md) | No lines. Just a cooler and a cone 15 ft apart, anywhere with room. |

## Kickball

Kickball reuses a corner of the field: home plate goes **on a corner**, and the **sideline and end line become the foul lines**. No new chalk is needed.

| Spot | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Home plate | On a field corner |
| 1st base | 20 yd (60 ft) along the sideline from home |
| 3rd base | 20 yd (60 ft) along the end line from home |
| 2nd base | 20 yd in from both 1st and 3rd. Check: about 28 yd 10 in straight from home. |
| Pitcher | 14 yd (42 ft) from home, straight toward 2nd |
| Bunt line | A 20 ft arc around home. Chalk it, or use two cones. |

Kicks go toward 2nd base, so deep kicks leave the chalked field over the far sideline. Pick a corner with open grass beyond that sideline.
