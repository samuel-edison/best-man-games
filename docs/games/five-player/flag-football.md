# Flag Football

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 5-player teams (5 v 5) |
| **Rounds** | 1 |
| **Teams** | Bottom 2 players are captains. Bottom player picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 5v5: 9 per winner · IDP Max: 10 (with bonus) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2025 |

## Setup

See **[Field Setup](field-setup.md)** for the shared field diagram and measurements.

- Same field as [Ultimate Frisbee](ultimate-frisbee.md): 50 yards long including 5-yard end zones, 25 yards wide.
- First down line is the 25-yard line, which is **midfield** (yards are counted from the end line).
- The starting spot is the 5-yard line, which is each team's **own goal line**.

## Rules

- No kickoffs. The ball starts at the 5-yard line.
- First down is at the 25-yard line.
- 3 Mississippis before rushing the QB.
- Immediate rushing is allowed if the ball changes hands to a different player after the snap.
- The offense may use as many linemen as they want, but linemen cannot receive or advance the ball.
    - Linemen must block with their hands behind their back.
    - Linemen must take off their flag before the play to show they are blocking only.
- First team to 3 scores wins.

## Bonus

- 1 point for MVP (most scores plus interceptions). Must be on the winning team.

## Supplies

- Pinnies, football, cones for the field, flags
