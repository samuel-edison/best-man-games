Standard soccer with two twists: no offsides, and no goalkeeper hands.

## Setup

Uses the shared 5v5 field and the same goals as [Handball](handball.md). See **[Field Setup](field-setup.md)** for the diagram and measurements.

## Rules

- Standard soccer rules, except for the two changes below.
- **No offsides.**
- **No hands for anyone.** Any player can play goalie, but the goalie can't use their hands either.
- **Handball:** using your arm below the shoulder to play the ball.
    - The ball touching your arm is fine, as long as you didn't clearly use the arm to control or redirect it.
- **Call your own fouls** (see [General Rules](../../rules/index.md#game-day-flow)).
- **Kickoff:** from the center dot to start each half and after every goal. The team that was just scored on kicks off.
- Two 12-minute halves. Switch ends at halftime. Most goals wins.

## Supplies

- Soccer ball
- 2 small goals, about 3 yards wide by 2 yards tall (the handball goals)
- Pinnies
- Stopwatch
