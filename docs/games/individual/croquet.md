# Croquet

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual |
| **Rounds** | 2 heats of 5 players |
| **Heats** | Hat draw |
| **Points** | Standard solo: 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0 · IDP Max: 9 |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | Not yet (new for 2026) |

Backyard croquet with the farm's set, using the standard 9-wicket layout.

## Setup

Set up the 9 wickets and 2 stakes in the standard double-diamond layout, using the diagram that came with the set. One stake is the **starting stake** and the other is the **turning stake**.

## Rules

- **Order of play:** follow the color order on the stakes.
- **Course:** start in front of the starting stake. Go through the wickets in order down to the turning stake and hit it. Then go through the wickets in order back up and hit the starting stake to finish.
- **Each turn** is one stroke, plus any bonus strokes you earn:
    - **Through a wicket** (in order): 1 bonus stroke. Two wickets in one stroke: 2 bonus strokes.
    - **Hitting the turning stake:** 1 bonus stroke.
    - **Hitting another ball:** 2 bonus strokes. You can only hit each ball once for bonus strokes until you go through your next wicket.
- **After hitting another ball,** you can do one of these with your first bonus stroke:
    - Place your ball touching theirs and hit yours, sending both balls.
    - Place your ball touching theirs, hold yours down with your foot, and hit it to send theirs away.
    - Play from a mallet head's length away from where your ball stopped.
- Bonus strokes don't stack. If you earn more on a bonus stroke, the new ones replace what you had left.
- **Out of bounds:** bring the ball back in a mallet head's length from where it went out.
- **Finishing:** once you hit the starting stake, your ball is out of the game. The next players keep going until all 5 have finished or the heat ends.

## Placement

- Within each heat, players are ranked by the order they finish.
- To rank all 10 players, line up the two heats by finishing position. Players who finished in the same position in different heats are ranked by **fewest turns taken**. For example, the two heat winners are 1st and 2nd overall, the two 2nd-place finishers are 3rd and 4th, and so on.
- Still tied: one stroke each from the starting stake toward the turning stake. Closest wins.

## Supplies

- The farm's croquet set (9 wickets, 2 stakes, mallets, balls)
- Someone keeping a turn count for each player
