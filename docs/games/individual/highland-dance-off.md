# Highland Dance-Off

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual (1 v 1) |
| **Rounds** | One song |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2024 (last place game) |

## Rules

- Play bagpipe music.
- The two dancers take turns doing their best Highland dance, battle style, until the song ends.
- The other 8 players vote. Majority wins.

## Supplies

- Speaker and bagpipe music
