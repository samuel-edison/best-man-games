# Stein Hold

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual (1 v 1 matchups) |
| **Rounds** | Each player holds once |
| **Matchups** | Hat draw |
| **Points** | 2024: each matchup winner gets 2 points, plus bonus · IDP Max: 4 |
| **Drinking** | No (steins hold water) |
| **Played** | 2024 (as "Stein Hui Hui," paired with [Chug Norris](chug-norris.md)) |

## Rules

- Both players in a matchup hold at the same time. Longest hold wins.
- Hold a full stein straight out in front of you with your **weak hand**.
- Keep a slight bend in your elbow. Don't lock it.
- You're out the moment:
    - Your holding arm drops noticeably below shoulder level
    - Your other arm goes behind your back to help
    - Any water spills

## Bonus

- 2 points for the longest hold of the night.

## Supplies

- Large glass steins filled with water to the pour line
- Stopwatch
