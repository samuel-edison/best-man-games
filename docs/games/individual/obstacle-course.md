# Obstacle Course

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual |
| **Rounds** | Each player attempts the course once. 1st place starts, ascending from there. |
| **Points** | Standard solo: 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0 · IDP Max: 11 (with bonus) |
| **Drinking** | Light (missed beer pong cups) |
| **Played** | 2024, 2025 |

## Rules

- Players complete a series of obstacles. Fastest time wins.
- Station the other players at the obstacles to help and watch for mistakes.
- If you don't complete an obstacle in the available tries, move on to the next one. Each incomplete obstacle adds **5 seconds**.

## Course

Start by the kickball area, near the disc golf hole 1 tree.

1. **Dizzy Bat:** spin 5 times (head down), then an underhand pitch is thrown. Ball must be hit or +5 seconds.
2. **Knife Throw:** into the hole 1 tree or a beer can. Stick one and move on, or +5 seconds if none.
3. **Medicine Ball Roll:** 20 feet long, between cones 5 feet wide, heading toward the camp fire. +5 seconds if the ball leaves the cones.
4. **Balance Beam:** stepping off is +5 seconds.
5. **Beer Pong:** 3 cups, 3 balls. Chug and flip any cup you don't make (no time penalty).
6. **Balance Beam** again.
7. **Medicine Ball Roll** again, different setup, same rules, heading toward the bell.
8. **Football Target Throw:** bottle bash target at 15 feet, overhand, one throw. Miss is +5 seconds.
9. **Ping Pong Ball on Spoon:** 10 feet, must complete. A drop is +5 seconds.
10. **Bell Ring** finish.

## Bonus

- 2 points for anyone completing all obstacles without error, except very last place (you can't just go slow on purpose).

## Supplies

- Dizzy bat and ball
- Knives and target
- Medicine ball
- Cones
- Balance beam
- Solo cups, beer, ping pong balls
- Spoon
- Football and bottle bash target
- Bell
- Stopwatch, paper and pen (error tally)
