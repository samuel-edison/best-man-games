# ChatGPT Spelling Bee

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual |
| **Rounds** | Until one player is left |
| **Points** | Standard solo: 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0 · IDP Max: 9 |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2025 |

## Rules

- **Numbering:** by current standings. 1st place is Player 1, 2nd place is Player 2, down to 10th place as Player 10.
- Stand in a circle in number order.
- **Pronouncer:** the player just before the speller holds the phone and reads the word. Player 10 pronounces for Player 1.
- After spelling, the speller takes the phone and pronounces for the next player.
- Spell a word wrong and you're out.
- If every remaining player spells their word correctly in a round, tell ChatGPT to increase the difficulty.
- Last player left wins. Players are ranked in reverse order of elimination.

## Prompt

> Run an elimination-style spelling bee for 10 numbered players. Only reveal one word at a time when I say 'next word.' Keep the difficulty constant until I tell you to increase it. Eliminate any player who gets a word wrong and never give them another word. Start with Player 1.

## Supplies

- Phone with ChatGPT
