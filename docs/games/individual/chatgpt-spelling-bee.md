# ChatGPT Spelling Bee

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | Individual |
| **Rounds** | Until failure |
| **Points** | Standard solo: 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0 · IDP Max: 9 |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2025 |

## Rules

- 1st place starts as Player 1, then go in ascending rank order.
- Everyone stands in a circle. The pronouncer is always the player before the speller (Player 10 pronounces first).
- After the speller attempts, the phone is passed to the speller, who becomes the next pronouncer.
- Spell a word wrong and you are eliminated.
- Difficulty increases if all remaining players complete a round without a mistake.

## Prompt

> Run an elimination-style spelling bee for 10 numbered players. Only reveal one word at a time when I say 'next word.' Keep the difficulty constant until I tell you to increase it. Eliminate any player who gets a word wrong and never give them another word. Start with Player 1.

## Supplies

- Phone with ChatGPT
