# Chariot Race

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | 2 heats per team (one with each partner driving) |
| **Teams** | [Picked Friday night](../../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams) from last year's rankings |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by fastest combined time) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | Not yet (new for 2026) |

A gladiator chariot race. One partner is the **driver** riding in a wheelbarrow, and the other is the **horse** pushing it.

## Setup

- Set a loop of cones around the [shared field](../five-player/field-setup.md), with a start/finish line at midfield.
- Each cone is a turn the chariot must go around on the outside.

## Rules

- **One team runs at a time**, timed from "go" until the wheelbarrow's front wheel crosses the finish line.
- **Heat 1:** one partner drives and the other is the horse. **Heat 2:** swap.
- **Driver:** must stay seated in the wheelbarrow the whole race. The driver can steer by calling directions, but can't touch the ground.
- **Horse:** pushes the wheelbarrow by the handles.
- **Missed cone:** go back and go around it before moving on.
- **Driver falls out:** stop, get back in at the spot where you fell, and keep going. The clock keeps running.
- **Order:** reverse standings, so the leading team runs last.
- Fastest **combined time** across both heats wins.
- **Ties:** one more heat each, driver of your choice. Fastest time wins.

## Supplies

- 1 wheelbarrow (same one for every team)
- Cones
- Stopwatch
