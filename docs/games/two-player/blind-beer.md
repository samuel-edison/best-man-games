# Blind Beer

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | 1 timed run per team |
| **Teams** | Bottom 5 players are captains. Bottom player picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by fastest time) |
| **Drinking** | Yes |
| **Played** | 2024, 2025 |

## Setup

- Mark a 20-foot diameter circle with cones.
- The team that just went helps with setup and spins. The last team helps the first team.

## Rules

- Both teammates are blindfolded and their 2 cans are placed inside the circle.
- Players are spun 5 times, then guided into the boundary.
- The timer starts once both players are in the boundary.
- You must stay in bounds. Non-players accurately tell you when to move back in.
- Blindfolds can come off once all cans are found, or you can chug what you find and keep the blindfold on.
- The timer stops once all drinks are finished.

## Supplies

- Cones, blindfolds, beer cans, stopwatch

## Year notes

- **2024:** 30-foot circle and **4 cans** per team. Points were by time bracket instead of placement: 5 points under 60 seconds, 3 under 120 seconds, 1 under 5 minutes, plus 1 bonus point for the fastest team in each bracket (IDP Max 6). Paired with [Egg Toss](egg-toss.md).
