# Beer Pong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | Bottom 5 players are captains and each picks a non-captain. Lowest captain picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | Yes |
| **Played** | 2024 (Kings of the Table), 2025 (round robin) |

## Setup

- Two tables if available.
- 6-cup racks filled with water, two balls per table.

## Rules

- 6-cup games with water cups.
- 1 drink per player per game.
- One drink per shot made, for both teammates.
- Losers must finish their own beer and prove it.
- Winners can carry their beer into the next game if they want.

## House rules

- 1 re-rack per game, at gentleman's discretion.
- One island per player per game (the player chooses the cup on a make).
- Balls back when both teammates make a cup.
- You must call "heating up" on your second make in a row to allow "fire" on the third.
- Once on fire, you shoot until you miss.
- Redemption on the final cup, unless the game ended on balls back, which is game over.
- In redemption, each player shoots until they miss.
- Party shot if the ball doesn't hit the ground.

## Supplies

- Two tables, ping pong balls, solo cups, beer

## Variant: Kings of the Table (2024)

- The two top-seeded captains face off first. Winner stays on until beaten.
- 2 points per player per win. The first team to 4 wins is crowned King (IDP Max 8).
- Minimum 2 beers per game per team.
- The losing team must finish the winning team's beers.
- Same house rules as above.

## One-on-one (tie breaker)

Used as the overall [tie breaker](../../rules/tiebreaker-last-place.md): each player gets two shots per turn, all other rules apply.
