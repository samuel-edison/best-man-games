# Beer Pong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | Bottom 5 players are captains and each picks a non-captain. Lowest captain picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | Yes |
| **Played** | 2024 (Kings of the Table), 2025 (round robin) |

## Setup

- Two tables if available.
- 6 cups per side in a triangle, filled with **water**. Two balls per table.
- Each player has their own beer for the game.

## Rules

- When the other team makes a cup, both of you take a drink of your own beer.
- **Losing team:** finish your beers and prove it (can upside down over your head).
- **Winning team:** you can carry your leftover beer into your next game.
- **Tied records:** decided by the head-to-head game.

## House rules

- **Re-rack:** 1 per team per game, at gentleman's discretion.
- **Island:** once per game, each player can call an island. On a make, the shooter chooses the cup.
- **Balls back:** if both teammates make a cup on the same turn, you get both balls back.
- **Heating up / on fire:** after your 2nd make in a row, you must call "heating up." If you then make a 3rd in a row, you're on fire and keep shooting until you miss.
- **Redemption:** when a team sinks the last cup, the other team gets redemption. Each player shoots until they miss.
    - No redemption if the last cup was sunk on a balls-back turn. That's game over.
    - **Overtime:** if redemption sinks every remaining cup, each team sets up 3 cups. The team that cleared its cups first (not the redemption team) shoots first.
- **Party shot:** if the ball doesn't hit the ground.

## Supplies

- Two tables, ping pong balls, solo cups, beer

## Variant: Kings of the Table (2024)

- The two top-seeded captains play first. Winner stays on until beaten.
- 2 points per player per win. First team to 4 wins is crowned King (IDP Max 8).
- Each team drinks at least 2 beers per game.
- The losing team finishes the winning team's beers.
- Same house rules as above.

## One-on-one (tie breaker)

Used as the overall [tie breaker](../../rules/tiebreaker-last-place.md): each player gets two shots per turn. All other rules apply.
