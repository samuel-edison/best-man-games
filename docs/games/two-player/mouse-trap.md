# Mouse Trap

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | [Picked Friday night](../../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams) from last year's rankings |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | Not yet (new for 2026) |

Like beer pong, but instead of shooting into a cup, your teammate traps the ball under one.

## Setup

- A beer pong table.
- 2 solo cups, one per team.
- 1 ping pong ball.
- **Positions:** teammates stand at opposite ends of the table. Each end has one player from each team, standing next to each other.
- Each team's cup sits upright at the edge of the table, at the receiving teammate's end.

## Rules

- A player sends the ball across the table toward their teammate at the other end.
- The teammate traps it by flipping their cup from the end of the table down over the ball.
- Each trap is 1 point.
- First team to **3 points** wins.
- **Tied records:** decided by the head-to-head game.

## Supplies

- Beer pong table, 2 solo cups, 1 ping pong ball
