# Cornhole

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | [Picked Friday night](../../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams) from last year's rankings |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | Not yet (new for 2026) |

Traditional doubles cornhole.

## Setup

- Set the two boards facing each other, **27 feet apart** from front edge to front edge.
- **Positions:** teammates stand at opposite boards. At each board, one player from each team stands side by side.
- Each team gets 4 bags of one color.

## Rules

- **Throwing:** underhand, from beside the board. Your feet can't pass the front edge of the board.
- **Innings:** the two players at one board alternate throws until all 8 bags are thrown. Then the players at the other board throw back.
- **First throw:** rock, paper, scissors (on shoot). After that, the team that scored in the last inning throws first.
- A bag that hits the ground before landing on the board doesn't count. Remove it before the next throw.

## Scoring

| Bag | Points |
| --- | --- |
| On the board at the end of the inning | 1 |
| In the hole | 3 |

- **Cancellation scoring:** points cancel each other out each inning. Only the team with more points scores, and only the difference. For example, 5 points to 3 points scores 2 for the first team.
- First team to **21 points** at the end of an inning wins.
- **Tied records:** decided by the head-to-head game.

## Supplies

- 2 cornhole boards
- 8 bags (4 each of two colors)
- Tape measure
