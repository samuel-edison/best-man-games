# Spikeball

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | Bottom 5 players are captains and each picks a non-captain. Lowest captain picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2024, 2025 |

Runs well side by side with [Kan Jam](kan-jam.md) using the same teams.

## Setup

- Set the net up in an open area.

## Rules

- Play to 13, win by 2.
- Rock, paper, scissors (on shoot) for first serve.
- Serves must be underhand.
- Each team gets up to 3 hits to send the ball back onto the net. No player can hit it twice in a row.
- Once the ball comes off the net, it's the other team's turn to return it.
- If the ball hits the net twice on its own, the other team wins the point.
- **Tied records:** decided by the head-to-head game.

## Supplies

- Spikeball net and ball

## Year notes

- **2024:** played to 15, win by 2. Not a full round robin: each team played twice on a set session schedule alongside [Beer Ball](beer-ball.md), and each player on a winning team got 5 points per win.
