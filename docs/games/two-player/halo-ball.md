# Halo Ball

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | Round robin: your team plays each other team once (4 games) |
| **Teams** | Bottom 5 players are captains and each picks a non-captain. Lowest captain picks first. |
| **Points** | Standard 2-player: 1st 9 per player, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2 (by most wins) |
| **Drinking** | No |
| **Played** | 2025 |

Official references: [setup guide](https://www.haloball.net/pages/set-up) and [rules](https://www.haloball.net/pages/rules).

## Setup

- Hang the hoop on the slack line between two trees, per the setup guide.
- Mark a 3-point line 10 feet from the hoop.

## Rules

- **No dribbling.** You get up to 2 steps with the ball. Pass to your teammate or off the tree to get your steps back.
- Traveling or dribbling is a turnover. The other team checks the ball at the 3-point line.
- **Checks:** start the game, and restart after every score or foul, with a check at the 3-point line.
- **Airball** (the shot touches nothing): the defense can grab it and shoot right away, without taking it back to the 3-point line.
- **No goaltending:** don't touch the rim or the slack line, and don't block a shot on its way down.
- **Tied records:** decided by the head-to-head game.

## Scoring

| Shot | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Layup or dunk | 1 |
| Midrange shot or alley-oop | 2 |
| 3-pointer | 3 |
| Goaltending (awarded to the shooting team) | 2 |

## Supplies

- Halo Ball set (slack line, ratchet, ball, tree protectors, hoop)
- Cones or marker for the 3-point line
