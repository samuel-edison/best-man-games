# Egg Toss

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | 2-player teams (5 teams of 2) |
| **Rounds** | 1 |
| **Teams** | [Picked Friday night](../../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams) from last year's rankings |
| **Points** | 2024: 1st 6, 2nd 4, 3rd 3, 4th 1, 5th 0 · IDP Max: 6 |
| **Drinking** | No (tie breaker only) |
| **Played** | 2024 (paired with [Blind Beer](blind-beer.md)) |

## Rules

- Teammates stand 20 feet apart. Each team has one egg.
- All teams toss at the same time and keep tossing back and forth until their egg breaks.
- Teams are ranked by how long their egg survives. Last egg standing wins.
- **Ties:** a chug-off with 2 drinks per team. Fastest team wins.

## Supplies

- 5 eggs (plus spares)
