## Picking teams (new this year)

Teams are picked from the **final 2025 rankings**, not the running totals on the day.

### 5v5 games

- The **top two players from 2025** are the captains.
- On Friday night, the two captains compete for who gets the first pick (competition to be decided). See the [Weekend Schedule](../rules/schedule.md).
- Players are picked in a **snake draft**: the first captain picks one player, then the other captain picks two in a row, and so on.

| Pick | Captain | Players taken |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Winner of the first-pick competition | 1 |
| 2 | Other captain | 2 |
| 3 | Winner | 2 |
| 4 | Other captain | 2 |
| 5 | Winner | 1 (last player) |

### 2v2 games

- The **top five players from 2025** are the captains, and each picks one partner.

## Points

The [standard point system](../rules/scoring.md):

- **5v5:** 9 per winner
- **2v2:** 1st 9, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2
- **Solo:** 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0
