## Picking teams (new this year)

Teams are picked Friday night from the **final 2025 rankings**, using the [general team-picking rules](../rules/scoring.md#picking-teams):

- **5v5:** the top 2 players from 2025 are captains. They compete for first pick, then run a snake draft.
- **2v2:** the top 5 players from 2025 are captains. Each picks one partner.

If a 2025 player can't attend, their replacement takes that player's 2025 placement (see [replacements](../rules/scoring.md#replacements)).

## Power Plays

Only the **bottom 5 players from 2025** get a [Power Play](../rules/power-plays.md), one each.

## Points

The [standard point system](../rules/scoring.md):

- **5v5:** 9 per winner
- **2v2:** 1st 9, 2nd 7, 3rd 5, 4th 2
- **Solo:** 1st 9, 2nd 8 … 9th 1, 10th 0
