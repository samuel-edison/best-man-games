The bachelor weekend edition, with a Highland theme.

## Rules specific to 2024

- The groom's word is law (unless it's a bad idea, then it's open to debate).
- **Points:** about 10 total points per game, split among the winners (usually 2 per winning player). Some games offered more.
- **Captains:** in later games, the **top** players by total points were captains (5 captains for 2-player games, 2 for 5-player games), with the lowest-point captain picking first. The early 5v5 games used preset teams that swapped between rounds.
- **Bonus chugs:** opened after lunch. 10 seconds to finish (8 seconds after the last day game). Duels used 10 paces and the winner took the whole pool.
- **Good vibes only:** starting an argument or fight cost 1 point per issue.
- **Puking:** cost 3 points and ended your bonus chugs.
- No [Power Plays](../rules/power-plays.md) this year.
