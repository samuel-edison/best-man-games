# Scoring & Teams

## Game formats

Every game is one of three formats. Each format has a max of five games in a year.

| Format | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **Individual (solo)** | Every player for themself |
| **2-player teams** | 5 teams of 2 |
| **5-player teams** | 5 v 5 |

In 2-player **round robin** games, every team plays each other team once. Points are awarded on overall record, not individual matches. Ties in record go to the head-to-head result.

## Standard point system

Each game awards about 45 total points, roughly 4.5 per player. Some games add bonus points.

| Format | Points |
| --- | --- |
| **5-player teams** | 9 pts per player on the winning team |
| **2-player teams** | 1st: 9 pts per player · 2nd: 7 · 3rd: 5 · 4th: 2 · 5th: 0 |
| **Individual** | 1st: 9 · 2nd: 8 · 3rd: 7 · 4th: 6 · 5th: 5 · 6th: 4 · 7th: 3 · 8th: 2 · 9th: 1 · 10th: 0 |

**IDP Max** on a game page is the most points one individual player can earn from that game, including bonuses.

!!! note "Points can change by year"
    This is the system introduced in 2025. 2024 used a smaller scale (about 10 points per game). Each [year page](../years/index.md) lists the points actually used.

## Picking teams

- The **lowest-ranked players are captains**, unless the game says otherwise.
    - 5-player games: bottom 2 players are captains.
    - 2-player games: bottom 5 players are captains.
- Captains pick in order from last place up (last place picks first).
- For back-to-back 2-player games run as a pair, teams stay together for both.

## Replacements

Rules that use the prior year's final rankings (captains, first pick, Power Plays) need all ten spots filled. If a player from the prior year can't attend, their replacement **takes that player's exact placement**. For example, the replacement for last year's 3rd place player is treated as 3rd place.

!!! note "2026 change"
    In 2026 teams are picked from the prior year's final rankings, with a duel and snake draft for 5v5 games. See the [2026 page](../years/2026.md#picking-teams-new-this-year).

## Awards

The winner is crowned Best Man, with a t-shirt customized for the year and/or their name on the victors plaque.
