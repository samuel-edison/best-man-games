# General Rules

These apply all weekend, every year, unless that [year's page](../years/index.md) says otherwise.

## House rules

- Be respectful to the farm and its owner.
- **Fire:** no playing with fire. The fire must be completely out, embers and all, before bed. Move all items away from the fire pit after use.
- **Cigarettes:** used cigarettes go in the designated container immediately, never on the grass.
- **Trash:** no throwing trash around. Clean up after each game so nobody has to do it hungover.
- **Phones:** no phones during the games, and preferably not at all, unless used for music, a game, or something cool.
- **Compete in everything:** play all games or don't get invited next year. No quitting early unless due to serious injury.

## What to bring

- Sleeping bag
- Tent (if sleeping outside)
- Koozie
- Costume (see that year's attire)
- 30 pack of light beer or equivalent
- Any other food or drink you want for yourself

## What will be provided

- Every meal from Friday dinner through Saturday dinner
- Water

## Game day flow

See the [Weekend Schedule](schedule.md) for the Friday-to-Sunday sequence of events.

- **Game order:** games are ordered by daylight and drinking level, with non-drinking games first. A solo game opens the day to establish initial rankings.
- **Game setup:** each game has a game maker and two assigned setup players. Failing to help with your assigned setup is a 1-point penalty.
- **Explanations:** each game is explained before play starts.
