# Weekend Schedule

The usual flow of the weekend. Exact times and the game lineup are on each [year's page](../years/index.md).

## Friday evening: arrival and setup

- **Arrive** and get settled in.
- **Set up the games before dark** so they are mostly ready to play Saturday morning. Each game's setup crew handles their own game (see [game setup](index.md#game-day-flow)).
- **Warm-up games:** play a game or two to get the weekend started.
- **Dinner** is served.
- **Pick teams** using the prior year's final rankings:
    - The **top two players** from last year compete to decide who gets **first pick** in the 5v5 snake draft.
    - Run the 5v5 draft and the 2v2 partner picks.
    - See the [2026 page](../years/2026.md#picking-teams-new-this-year) for how the draft works.
- **Movie night** to close out the evening.

## Saturday: game day

- **Sunrise:** wake up.
- **Breakfast**, then get started on the games right away.
- **Main competition:** play through the lineup, ordered by daylight and drinking level, non-drinking games first.
- **Goal: all scored games finished before 5 PM.**
- **After the main competition:**
    - Final tally, [tie breaker](tiebreaker-last-place.md) if needed, the last place game, and the Best Man is crowned.
    - **Dinner.**
    - **Light games** as desired, just for fun.

## Sunday morning: pack out

- **Full clean up** of the site. Leave the farm better than we found it.
- **Pack out** and head home.
