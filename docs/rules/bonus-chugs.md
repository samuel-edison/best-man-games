# Bonus Chugs & Beer Duels

## Anytime bonus chugs

- During any post-game intermission, you may declare a chug for **1 bonus point**.
- Finish in **10 seconds**, or **lose 1 point**.
- Once per intermission.
- Failed chugs add a 5-second penalty.
- If more than one player declares in the same intermission, it becomes a **beer duel**: fastest time gets the point, slowest time loses a point.

## Beer duel

1. Duelers start back-to-back.
2. On "Ready, go," each takes five paces.
3. On "Draw," they turn and face each other.
4. Chugging and the timer start on the first can click.
5. All standard chug rules still apply.

## Proving a finished chug

Turn the can or cup upside down over your head. There should be minimal, slow drip at most.

!!! note "Year differences"
    2024: bonus chugs only opened after lunch, with a 10-second limit (8 seconds after the last day game), duels used 10 paces, and the duel winner took the whole pool of points. Puking removed your bonus chug privileges.
