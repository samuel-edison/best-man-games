# Power Plays

Only the **bottom 5 players from the prior year's final rankings** receive a Power Play. Each gets **one**, and it can only be used one time.

**Picking:** on Friday night, those 5 players pick from the list below in reverse order of last year's finish. Last place picks first, then 9th, 8th, 7th, and 6th. Each Power Play can only be taken once.

If one of those players can't attend, their replacement takes their place in the rankings and gets the Power Play (see [replacements](scoring.md#replacements)).

| # | Power Play | What it does | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Mulligan** | Choose a play where you get an extra attempt (bad throw, missed kick, etc.) | Cannot be used in the Obstacle Course. Coming back to life after being knocked out does not count. |
| 2 | **Forced Substitution** | Force a player to sit out for one play | Cannot be used in solo events |
| 3 | **Flex Position** | Place yourself in any position at any time (throw for your partner, kick for your team) | |
| 4 | **Time Freeze** | Stop the game to "rewind" the previous play. The entire play is redone. | Must be called immediately after the play. No late calls. |
| 5 | **Sabotage Token** | Choose one opposing player. They must perform the next play with a disadvantage (weak-hand throw, opposite-foot kick, etc.) | |
| 6 | **Shield Token** | Cancels any power play used against you or your team | |
| 7 | **Perfect Captain** | For one team game, you become a captain regardless of ranking | You replace the top-ranked captain (e.g. 9th place in a 5v5 game) |
| 8 | **Free Chug** | Prevents you from losing a penalty point on a chug | Must be declared before the chug |
| 9 | **Power Point** | In a point-based game, declare the next scoring opportunity to receive 1 additional point | Not for 5v5 games. Only awarded on a successful score. |
| 10 | **Hold My Beer** | Force a player to play beer-in-hand until they finish it | The game stops to get the beer. Drinking cannot start until the game resumes. |

Introduced in 2025, when every player got one. From 2026 on, only the prior year's bottom 5 get one.
